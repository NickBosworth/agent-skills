# AI search visibility without speculative hacks

## Foundation
Treat generated search answers as another discovery surface whose inclusion is controlled by its provider. Improve useful original information, accurate facts, ordinary technical accessibility and clear source material. Do not promise a citation or confuse a chatbot’s answer with a reproducible ranking measurement. [G06, B03]

Google’s current AI guidance does not require special AI schema or dedicated AI text files. It explicitly says `llms.txt` does not positively or negatively affect Google Search visibility/ranking; other consumers may have separate reasons to use it. Do not sell arbitrary chunk sizes, manufactured entities, repeated brand mentions or fan-out pages as mandatory AI optimisation. [G06]

## Content decisions
Ask whether the site supplies a worthwhile answer, verified evidence and a usable next step. Provide natural definitions, relevant qualifications and appropriate media where they aid understanding. Answer-first structure can be useful editorial judgement; it is not a guaranteed citation recipe. Do not rewrite every page into disconnected fragments to appease a speculative machine preference. [B03, B08]

Separate real product/local/organisation facts from promotional claims. Do not create fake third-party comparisons, tests, consensus or authoritative quotations. Model-generated text needs verification and editorial accountability. A citation is not evidence that the cited text is factually correct. [G07]

## Controls and ownership
Search crawling, model training, user-initiated fetching, snippets and paywall access can be controlled through different products and mechanisms. Verify each actual crawler/product; do not assume one bot exclusion controls every AI surface. Preserve owner policy choices and security boundaries. Excluding a snippet is not a method for protecting confidential information. [G04, G09, B04]

## Measurement
Use current Google/Bing reports when available and keep their metric definitions intact; see [measurement](measurement.md). A manual question panel can be an explicitly labelled exploratory observation, with prompt, date, system, locale, repeated-run variability and citations captured. Do not invent a universal “AI share of voice” from a few hand-picked answers or equate observed citation frequency with revenue.

## Evaluation of third-party claims
For an AEO/GEO recommendation, ask: which provider and feature; what primary evidence; what tested conditions; what confounders; what downside; and what would falsify it? Separate a vendor’s hypothesis, experiment and marketing claim. Reject unsupported guarantees, deceptive content and changes that worsen the real page. An honest experiment may still be worth testing when the risk is low and measurement is explicit. [G08]

Sources: G04, G06–G09, B03, B04, B08. This pack deliberately rejects universal citation/ranking formulas.
