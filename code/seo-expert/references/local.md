# Local and service-area SEO

## Establish the real operating footprint
Ask or inspect verified business facts: trading name, public contact details, staffed locations where applicable, service areas, opening hours, actual services, booking/contact flow and relevant credentials. Do not create fictional branches, addresses, employees, local reviews or location expertise. A service-area business and a walk-in branch need different representations.

Review the website alongside authorised Google Business Profile and Bing Places information, using current product policies before any account action. Profiles should match reality; category, business-name and eligibility rules need current platform verification. A website audit does not authorise changing profiles. [G44, B06]

## Location pages need a reason to exist
A genuine location or distinct service market may merit a page when the business can supply useful local details: services available there, access/visit information, actual hours, coverage limitations, proof of work, relevant staff or a clear contact route. Substituting city names into identical text is not a useful local strategy and can resemble doorway creation. [G02]

For a service-area page, explain what is genuinely delivered there. Do not imply a physical office when none exists. Consolidate weak duplicated place pages only after examining user value, traffic, conversions and real service differences. Demand research does not authorise fabricating presence.

## Page and journey checks
Verify the route from local intent to the correct contact or booking destination. Make practical details easy to find on mobile. Compare visible facts with relevant structured data and approved profile data. Check stale holiday hours, broken telephone links, misleading maps and redirects to the wrong branch. Avoid publishing private home addresses or staff details just to complete a field. [G34]

## Reputation
Recommend legitimate ways to make it easy for real customers to provide feedback under the platform’s current rules. Do not buy reviews, write them on a customer’s behalf, suppress criticism through review gating or trade rewards for a fabricated sentiment. Responses and claimed service quality should be accurate. [G32]

## Measurement and expectation
Local visibility depends on context; Google describes relevance, distance and prominence among its local considerations. Do not promise to overcome geography by repeating city names or acquiring a special schema type. Observe qualified contacts/bookings and location-specific performance when legitimately measurable, with privacy safeguards. [G44]

## Deliverables
Provide a real-location/service inventory, inconsistency log, location-page opportunities with unique evidence, approved-profile change requests, mobile conversion checks and a measurement plan. Profile exports or changes require connected access and owner permission; otherwise identify exactly what remains unverified.

Sources: G02, G32, G34, G44, B06. Bing Places details must be checked against current local-product documentation at execution.
