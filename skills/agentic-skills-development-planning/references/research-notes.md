# Provenance and limits

The main workflow was retained from the original package. Supporting references,
templates and scenarios were newly reconstructed on 2026-10-10 because the public
and installed copies lacked them. This is not a recovery of the original release.

The instruction design is repository-grounded engineering synthesis, not a universal
industry standard or deterministic enforcement system. Real host permissions govern
writes; skill prose cannot confer permission or guarantee behaviour.

The [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification) supports the
self-contained package and staged resource loading. Treat changing host discovery
paths as documentation-supported until observed in the exact host/version. Test
planning behaviour with the supplied scenarios; their presence does not mean a model
has executed them. Do not attribute a historical motivation without actual evidence.
