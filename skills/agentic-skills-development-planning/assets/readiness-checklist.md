# Readiness checklist

- Outcome and supplied acceptance criteria are concrete and traceable.
- Applicable rules and rationale have been inspected; material conflicts are resolved.
- Affected consumers and real compatibility/data risks are identified.
- Selected design, actual/proposed paths, ordered work and dependencies are explicit.
- Relevant verification and safe execution environment are established.
- Test sequencing and required documentation are settled; broader audit is separate.
- Migration, recovery and stop conditions address the actual risk.
- Remaining unknowns are bounded; required prerequisites are not silently assumed.
- The plan revision is clear. Readiness, approval and completed verification are distinct.

An unchecked material item means draft/investigation readiness, not feature readiness.
