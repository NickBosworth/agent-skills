# Implementation plan

## Outcome and evidence
State the user outcome, baseline/revision, governing requirements, source evidence,
non-goals, assumptions and unresolved material questions.

## Selected decisions
Explain the chosen approach, preserved rules, affected consumers, compatibility and
why realistic alternatives were rejected. Distinguish approved decisions from proposals.

## Ordered work
For each step give actual/proposed paths, concrete change, dependencies, observable
acceptance evidence and checkpoint. Include migration/recovery only where needed.

## Verification and documentation
Record safe commands, test sequence, required docs, separately authorized broader
audit, baseline failures and unavailable checks. State stop/replan conditions.

## Readiness and authority
Name the plan revision and whether it is a blocked draft, ready plan or approved
implementation. Do not imply approval or completion from the existence of this record.
