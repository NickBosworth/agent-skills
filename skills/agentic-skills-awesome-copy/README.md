# agentic-skills · Awesome Copy

Version **1.0.2** · Licence **CC0-1.0**

Write titles and copy with a clear idea, accurate detail and a voice that fits
the reader. Improve generic wording by editing meaning, structure and rhythm.
Keep strong original language and the qualifications that make a claim accurate.

## Installation and use

Install the entire `agentic-skills-awesome-copy` folder in your host's documented
skill directory. Keep the references, examples, evaluation scenarios and licence
together. Discovery and invocation syntax depend on the host; confirm the package
is listed, then use its picker or `$` invocation.

```text
Use $agentic-skills-awesome-copy to rewrite this homepage copy. Keep the supplied
facts and calm voice. Give me the finished headline, support line and CTA.
```

```text
Use $agentic-skills-awesome-copy to propose five distinct titles for this essay.
Each must be under 60 characters. Recommend the one that best fits its argument.
```

```text
Use $agentic-skills-awesome-copy to critique this newsletter for generic phrasing
and weak ideas. Do not rewrite it yet; identify the most useful changes.
```

Read [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) for the workflow. The package covers commercial and
editorial copy, emails, posts and product text. Code/API documentation alone,
visual redesign and authorship detection are outside its scope. It works offline
from supplied text and needs no helper, subscription or detector service.

## Resources and evidence

- [Editing practice](references/editing.md) explains observable habits and
  context-sensitive repairs without a rigid blacklist.
- [Titles and formats](references/titles-and-formats.md) adapts the work to its
  actual surface, including formal and international writing.
- [Eleven original examples](examples/worked-examples.md) demonstrate useful
  rewrites and restraint with wholly synthetic material.
- [Research basis](references/research-basis.md) records 14 primary sources,
  editorial inferences, counterevidence and limits, reviewed on 2026-10-12.
- [Behavioral evaluation](evals/README.md) defines how to assess the
  [scenarios](evals/scenarios.json); these are not completed host/model benchmarks.

Structural validation checks the package, links and metadata. It does not prove
that readers will prefer every output, that a host will discover the skill, or
that copy will increase conversions. This is a new package with no legacy alias.
