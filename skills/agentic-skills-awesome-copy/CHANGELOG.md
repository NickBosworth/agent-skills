# Changes

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-12

Initial self-contained package for writing, revising and critiquing natural,
specific titles and copy. Adds a contextual editing workflow, voice and rhythm
guidance, channel-specific decisions, 11 original synthetic examples, a research
synthesis of 14 primary sources and behavioral evaluation scenarios. No automated
authorship detector, banned-word checker or measured writing-quality claim.
