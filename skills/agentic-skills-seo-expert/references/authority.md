# Legitimate authority, links and reputation

## Earn relevance rather than manufacture signals
Recommend work the organisation can honestly stand behind: useful original resources, transparent methodology, helpful tools, accurate listings, expert contributions and legitimate partnerships. Coverage and mentions should arise from genuine relevance; no number of prestigious names inserted into prose proves expertise.

A backlink tool provides a partial vendor dataset. Record provider, date and coverage. Its authority, toxicity or difficulty scores are vendor metrics, not Google/Bing scores. Use them as investigation leads, not instructions to buy links or remove assets. Do not infer a penalty merely from an alarming vendor dashboard. [G08]

## Audit what matters
Inspect a sample of relevant referring pages, destination status, link context, genuine relationship and recoverable errors. Broken inbound links may justify fixing the referenced asset or redirecting to an actually equivalent page, not capturing every unrelated old URL. Compare evidence to the business’s known history before alleging abuse.

## Non-negotiable exclusions
Do not buy/sell ranking credit, operate private link networks, create fake reviews, mass-spam forums, generate deceptive expert biographies, scrape and republish others’ work, or run unsolicited automated outreach. Sponsorship and legitimate advertising are business decisions; qualifying links appropriately is different from disguising payment. Current spam policy applies to the actual practice, not a vendor’s euphemism. [G02]

## Removals and disavow
Never submit a disavow or mass removal request based only on a proprietary toxicity score. Require current official guidance, evidence of the actual problem, owner approval and a rollback/record plan. This pack intentionally does not supply a “clean all toxic links” automation. A suspicious link’s existence alone does not establish that a site owner caused it or needs action.

## Proposed outreach
An agent may prepare a transparent, personalised proposal within scope, but sending it requires authorisation and valid contact/privacy handling. Do not impersonate a customer, researcher or independent reviewer. Do not promise placement or ranking. Judge success by legitimate relevance, qualified engagement and business outcomes, not an arbitrary link quota.

Sources: G02, G08, G18. The risk controls and suggested evidence approach are pack policy.
